FAQ

Common questions, straight answers

Everything you need to know about how AgroAI works.

Do I need a soil sensor to use AgroAI?

No — you can manually enter your NPK, pH, moisture and temperature readings from any source. The IoT sensor integration is optional but gives you the most accurate, real-time results automatically.

Why does the confidence score never reach 100%?

We deliberately cap confidence at 97% using isotonic calibration and conformal prediction sets. Soil and weather are too complex for any model to be perfectly certain — and we think honesty matters more than impressive-looking numbers.

What crops does the engine support?

The recommendation engine covers 30+ crop types common to Maharashtra — including rice, wheat, sugarcane, cotton, jowar, bajra, groundnut, onion and several pulses. The disease-detection module supports corn, rice, wheat, millet and sugarcane leaves.

Is the API free to use?

The API is currently in research preview. Core endpoints are open for testing. Commercial licensing terms are being finalised — reach out if you'd like early access for your agtech project.

How does the AI second-opinion work?

After the ML model scores each candidate crop, an agronomy-tuned LLM independently reviews the top result against regional context, season and soil conditions. If the two disagree on a key point, both views are surfaced in the response — not silently merged.

How is user authentication handled?

We use Supabase for authentication, which means your account and farm data are stored securely with 24/7 availability — independent of whether the local API services are running. Your data is always accessible.

Ready to put your soil data to work?

Get a calibrated crop recommendation in under two seconds. No account required to try the public dashboard.
